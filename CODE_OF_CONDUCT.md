# Code of conduct

djcast is made by and for DJs and radio people of every background and level of experience.
Everyone who takes part in this project (issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews) is
expected to help keep it friendly and useful.

## Expected
- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to streaming, DJ software or code.
- Assume good intent; ask before concluding.
- Give and take criticism about the work, not about the person.
- Respect people's time: search before asking, and say thanks when something helped.

## Not accepted
- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background or skills.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, or encouraging others to do any of this.
- Publishing someone's private information (addresses, stream passwords, accounts) without
  their permission.
- Spam, or using the project to advertise unrelated products or services.
- Sharing pirated music, cracked software or keys taken from DJ software.

## Enforcement
The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock conversations, and block people who do not
follow this code, in proportion to what happened. To report a problem privately, use the
[private reporting form](https://github.com/danmarinescu79/djcast/security/advisories/new)
(say that it is a conduct report) or GitHub's *Report content* option on the comment.
Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
